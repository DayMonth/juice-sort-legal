Support — Juice Sort

App: Juice Sort   Bundle ID: com.water.juice   Platforms: iOS, Android

Juice Sort is a single-player, fully offline bottle-sorting puzzle game. This page covers the questions we get most often and tells you how to reach us if something goes wrong.

Contact us: whiteblacktasks@gmail.com
We aim to reply within 3 business days. Please tell us your device model, operating system version, and the app version shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The Game is fully playable offline. It has no account system and makes no network requests of its own.

How is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new device?

Progress is stored only in the Game's private storage on your device: the current level, your daily streak record and claimed streak rewards, your booster inventory, and your sound / music / vibration preferences. There is no cloud save and no account, so progress cannot be transferred between devices or recovered after the app is deleted.

I lost my progress after reinstalling. Can you restore it?

Unfortunately not. Because nothing is uploaded anywhere, we have no copy of your progress to restore. Deleting the app (or clearing its storage on Android) permanently erases the save. On iOS, if you have device backups enabled, restoring the device backup may bring the save back; that backup is controlled by Apple, not by us.

What are the boosters (reset / undo / add bottle)?

Boosters help you out of a tight spot. Each one has a limited inventory; using one consumes a charge. When a booster's inventory reaches zero, the Game offers a "watch a video" prompt that refills it. In version 1.0 that prompt is a local placeholder — it grants the booster immediately and does not play a real advertisement, contact an ad network, or send any data. If a later version introduces real rewarded advertising, the Privacy Policy will be updated before that version ships.

The video prompt does nothing / no ad appeared.

That is expected in version 1.0. The prompt is a placeholder and the booster is granted instantly without any video. Nothing is broken and no data leaves your device.

Is there a time limit on a level?

No. Levels have no countdown — take as long as you like.

How difficult is the game? Why did the level style change?

The main campaign gets harder as you progress: every 10th level is a Hard level and every 50th is a Super Hard level, shown with a different frame colour and a short intro before the level starts. Tall bottles also appear in the campaign from level 21 onward, roughly once every five levels.

What is the daily streak and the monthly trophy?

Playing on consecutive days builds a streak and unlocks streak rewards, including a gift. The achievement screen also tracks a 12-month trophy set, one trophy shape per month, kept for the current year. Missing a day resets the streak counter; rewards already claimed stay claimed.

How do I turn off sound, music, or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen or from inside a level and toggle sound effects, music, and vibration. The choices persist across sessions.

Text or buttons look wrong on my device / the layout is cut off.

Please send us a screenshot with your device model and OS version. Layout issues on unusual aspect ratios (foldables, tablets, very tall screens) are the ones we can act on fastest with a screenshot.

Does the Game collect my data or show ads?

No. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Reporting a bug

To help us reproduce the problem, please include:

  • device model and operating system version (for example "iPhone 15, iOS 26.1" or "Pixel 8, Android 15");
  • the Game version and build number shown in Settings;
  • the level or pack you were on, and what happened versus what you expected;
  • a screenshot or screen recording if the issue is visual;
  • whether restarting the app changes anything.

Send it to whiteblacktasks@gmail.com with the subject "Juice Sort Bug Report". A pre-filled draft is also available from Settings → Feedback inside the Game.

Feature requests

Ideas are welcome at the same address. We read everything but cannot promise a reply to every suggestion, or that a suggestion will be implemented.
